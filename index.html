Our planned process

Test first. Match the equipment. Verify the installation.

This approach is intended to reduce oversizing, undersizing and unnecessary equipment.

Understand the home

Identify the service address, utility or well, household size, bathrooms, high-flow fixtures and the homeowner’s primary concerns.

Verify the water

Review utility information and conduct appropriate onsite or laboratory testing before making contaminant-specific recommendations.

Confirm the system

A qualified installation professional verifies plumbing, pressure, flow, drainage, electrical access and available equipment space before ordering.

Common questions

Before you join

Are you currently scheduling installations?

Not yet. Front Range Water Pros is currently collecting homeowner interest while building its supplier, testing, insurance and licensed-installation relationships. Joining the list reserves no appointment and creates no purchase obligation.

Will the questionnaire tell me exactly what to buy?

No. The questionnaire supports initial qualification. Final equipment selection should follow water testing and verification of plumbing size, pressure, flow, drainage, electrical access and available installation space.

Do all Denver-area homes need a water softener?

No. Water characteristics vary by utility, source, neighborhood and season. Softening should be considered using measured hardness, the homeowner’s concerns and the expected benefit.

Who will perform plumbing installation?

Any plumbing installation offered through the future service will be assigned to an appropriately licensed installation professional. Final responsibilities and warranties will be identified in the applicable written agreement.

Will joining the list affect my credit?

No. The waitlist does not involve financing, a credit check, payment information, a deposit or a contract.
